FIX 1 — floorsServed detecting 3 instead of 2
In egress-sign-generator.ts, detectFloorCount
is counting 3 floors for Fox Hill (2-floor building).

The 3rd floor is 'UNSPECIFIED FLOOR' or a blank
entry from the egress generator's own previous run.

Fix detectFloorCount to only count floors that
match a real floor pattern:

  SELECT COUNT(DISTINCT floor) as floor_count
  FROM signs
  WHERE job_id = $1
  AND floor IS NOT NULL
  AND floor != ''
  AND floor ~ '^(LEVEL|FLOOR|FL|STORY)\s+\d+$'
  OR floor ~ '^\d+$'
  -- Only floors matching 'LEVEL 1', 'Floor 2', '3' etc
  -- Excludes 'UNSPECIFIED FLOOR', 'EVAC-L1', blanks

Also exclude signs the egress generator itself
inserted in previous runs — they may have wrong
floor values that corrupt the count:

  AND source != 'rules'
  -- Only count floors from extracted room signs
