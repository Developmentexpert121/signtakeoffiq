Classification logic:
  1. Check title against SKIP keywords first
     → if match: skip this sheet entirely
  2. Check title against FLOOR_PLAN keywords
     AND not in title_exclusions
     → if match: FLOOR_PLAN
  3. Check title against SIGN_DETAILS keywords
     → if match: SIGN_DETAILS
  4. Check title against SPECIALTY_SIGNAGE
     → if match: SPECIALTY_SIGNAGE
  5. Check title against EGRESS
     → if match: EGRESS
  6. No match → send to Gemini for classification
     (fallback for unusual naming)

  → Do NOT process
  → Do NOT show in Plans tab
