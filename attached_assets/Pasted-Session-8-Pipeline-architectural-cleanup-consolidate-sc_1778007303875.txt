Part 1 — Consolidate schedule sign arrays (pipeline.ts only)
Right now three things hold schedule sign data:

signSchedule[] — Gemini-extracted entries, populated in Step 3a
scheduleSignRows[] — what Step 9 reads, populated inconsistently across 4 different paths
References to sign_schedule_entries table that doesn't exist

The fix: Make signSchedule[] the single source of truth. Remove scheduleSignRows[] entirely. At Step 9, read directly from signSchedule[] and convert to sign rows in one place:
typescript// ONE place where schedule entries become sign rows
if (pipelineStrategy === "schedule_primary" && signSchedule.length > 0) {
  const scheduleSignInserts = signSchedule.map(entry => ({
    id: newId("sign"),
    jobId,
    tenantId,
    roomId: matchedRoomId(entry.roomNumber) ?? null,
    sheetId: String(_3aAnchorSheetId ?? ""),
    signType: entry.signType,
    qty: entry.quantity ?? 1,
    ruleRef: "schedule",
    color: null,
    confidence: "1.000",
    status: "extracted",
    source: "schedule",
    message: [entry.roomNumber, entry.roomName, entry.signType, entry.message]
      .filter(Boolean).join(" | ") || undefined,
    level: entry.floor ? parseScheduleFloor(entry.floor) : null,
  }));
  
  await db.insert(signsTable).values(scheduleSignInserts);
  logger.log(`[Step 9] schedule_primary: inserted ${scheduleSignInserts.length} schedule signs`);
}
Delete all the scattered scheduleSignRows.push() calls across Steps 3a, 4b, 4d, 4d-2. Delete scheduleSignRows declaration. Delete dedicatedFileTypeDefMatch — it becomes unnecessary.
Part 2 — Clear data flow documentation
Add a comment block at the top of processJob() explaining the pipeline strategy paths:
typescript/*
 * PIPELINE DATA FLOW
 * 
 * schedule_primary (has sign schedule with 100+ entries):
 *   Step 3a → signSchedule[] (Gemini extracts from sign schedule PDF)
 *   Step 3  → floor plan vision runs for egress rooms + coordinates only
 *   Step 3b → coordinate matching: schedule rooms get x,y from floor plans
 *   Step 9  → signSchedule[] → signs table (direct, no rules engine)
 *   Step 9  → rules engine runs R9/R11/R13/R16 only (egress)
 * 
 * floor_plan_primary (no sign schedule):
 *   Step 3  → floor plan vision runs fully → extractedRooms[]
 *   Step 9  → rules engine runs fully → signs table
 * 
 * hybrid (sign schedule with <100 entries):
 *   Both paths run, results merged
 * 
 * egress_only (no floor plans):
 *   Step 9  → egress rules only
 */
